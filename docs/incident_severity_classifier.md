\# Incident Severity Classification (Protocol Qv Rev 7.1)



This artifact classifies incident severity as minor, serious, or systemic

based on observable aggregation of harm after deployment.



It does not predict harm, assign blame, or infer intent.



Severity escalation is based only on:

\- repeated effects,

\- correlation across affected users,

\- persistence over time,

\- asymmetric impact on constrained populations.



If inputs are incomplete, confidence is downgraded and unknowns are preserved.
