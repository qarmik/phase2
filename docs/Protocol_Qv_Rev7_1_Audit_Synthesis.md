\# Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 — Audit \& Regulator Synthesis



\## Purpose

Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 is a post-incident accountability system. It does not predict harm, assign blame, or guarantee enforcement outcomes. Its purpose is to ensure that, after an incident, the presence or prevention of accountability is explicit, timestamped, and auditable.



\## What the Protocol Does

\- Freezes the incident boundary and evidence set.

\- Classifies evidence deterministically.

\- Records responsibility limits through a formal lattice.

\- Accepts or rejects counterfactual claims mechanically.

\- Produces a unified incident record.

\- Preserves unknowns as first-class artifacts.

\- Classifies incident severity from observed outcomes.

\- Packages regulator handoff materials.

\- Enables cross-incident comparison.

\- Provides a public replay path that either reproduces the outcome or emits an explicit refusal.



Each step produces an artifact or an explicit refusal.



\## What Is Auditable Today

A regulator or third party can independently verify:

\- What evidence was considered and when it was frozen.

\- How responsibility was bounded.

\- Why certain counterfactuals were rejected.

\- How severity was classified based on observed outcomes.

\- What information was handed to regulators.

\- Whether replay is possible and, if not, why it is blocked.



All decisions are evidence-bound and deterministic.



\## What the Protocol Explicitly Refuses to Do

Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 does not:

\- Predict future harm.

\- Rank individuals or assign intent.

\- Override human or regulator authority.

\- Proceed when evidence is missing, altered, or blocked.

\- Circumvent sovereign, legal, or vendor-imposed constraints.



When such conditions exist, the protocol emits an explicit refusal code.



\## Role of Refusal

Refusal is not failure. It is an auditable terminal state that documents why accountability could not proceed further. This prevents silent failure and preserves institutional traceability.



\## Scope Boundary

Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 operates only after an incident has occurred. It is not a risk scoring system, a design-time governance framework, or a preventative control.



\## Summary

Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 ensures that accountability outcomes—or the prevention of those outcomes—are visible, reviewable, and contestable using concrete artifacts rather than narrative explanation.
