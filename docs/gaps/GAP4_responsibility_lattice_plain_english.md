1\. What was attempted



To make responsibility explicit across roles involved in an incident, instead of attributing accountability to a single individual.



2\. What was actually built



A responsibility lattice artifact mapping roles, authority, control points, and override limits for a specific intervention.



3\. Why this matters in the real world



Institutions often diffuse responsibility. This artifact shows exactly who could do what, and who could not.



4\. What went wrong or was unclear



Initial attempts conflated roles and persons. This was corrected by enforcing role-based accountability only.



5\. What I can now explain out loud



I can explain who had decision authority, who could override, and where accountability stops.



6\. What remained unresolved



Cross-incident role consistency is handled later (GAP 9).
