\# Day 40 — Priority 2: Audit / Regulator Synthesis  

Plain-English Record



\## What was done

We produced a single document that explains Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 to auditors and regulators in clear terms, without adding new rules or reinterpretations.



\## What can be audited now

A regulator can see:

\- What evidence was used.

\- When boundaries were frozen.

\- How responsibilities were limited.

\- Why certain claims were accepted or rejected.

\- Whether replay is possible and, if not, the exact reason why.



\## What the system refuses to do

The system will not guess, predict, or proceed without evidence. When it cannot continue, it records that refusal explicitly.



\## Why this matters

This approach prevents silent failure. It ensures that limits, blocks, and non-cooperation are visible and reviewable instead of hidden.



\## Bottom line

Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 does not promise accountability. It guarantees that the absence or prevention of accountability is documented in a way regulators can examine.
