1\. What was attempted



To classify incident severity, including slow-motion or aggregated harm, without prediction or blame.



2\. What was actually built



A severity classification artifact producing minor, serious, or systemic outcomes with explicit justification.



3\. Why this matters in the real world



Regulators ask whether an incident is systemic. This artifact answers that using observed outcomes only.



4\. What went wrong or was unclear



Severity was previously implied rather than codified.



5\. What I can now explain out loud



I can explain why an incident is classified as systemic without claiming intent or causality.



6\. What remained unresolved



Severity classification does not trigger enforcement by itself.
