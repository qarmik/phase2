\# Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 — Temporal Limits \& Holding Pattern Declaration



\## Status



This document records a \*\*deliberate holding pattern\*\* under \*\*Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 (Frozen)\*\*.



It is an interpretive boundary declaration, not a corrective action and not a roadmap.



---



\## Context



Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 is structurally complete and has been executed correctly across GAPs 1–10.



During post-completion analysis, materials were generated and published identifying a \*\*structural insufficiency\*\* (referred to as GAP 25) related to \*\*temporal evidence coalescence\*\*.



Those materials represent:



\- failure detection,

\- adversarial analysis,

\- and decision inputs.



They do \*\*not\*\* represent:

\- the opening of Rev 7.2,

\- an enforceable new GAP,

\- or a schema-backed protocol extension.



They are preserved intentionally as part of the accountability record.



---



\## Nature of the Limitation



Under certain conditions, evidence fragments that only become interpretable \*\*after\*\* an incident boundary freeze may alter how earlier accountability artifacts are understood.



This limitation arises from:

\- the temporal structure of the protocol itself,

\- not from incorrect execution,

\- not from missing artifacts,

\- not from refusal handling,

\- and not from external non-cooperation.



It is a \*\*known temporal limit\*\* of Rev 7.1.



---



\## Current Position



At this time:



\- Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 remains \*\*complete and frozen\*\*.

\- GAPs 1–10 remain \*\*closed and authoritative\*\*.

\- GAP 25 is \*\*acknowledged as a detected insufficiency\*\*, but is \*\*not actionable\*\* within Rev 7.1.

\- No schema, control, or mechanism currently exists to address this without altering doctrine.



Accordingly, \*\*no corrective action is being taken\*\*.



This non-action is \*\*intentional, explicit, and recorded\*\*.



---



\## Meaning of This Holding Pattern



This holding pattern means:



\- Known limits are declared rather than concealed.

\- Prior GAP 25 materials remain visible as evidence of detection.

\- No remediation is promised or implied.

\- No future revision is assumed.



The protocol continues to function correctly \*\*within its declared bounds\*\*.



---



\## Conditions for Exit



This holding pattern remains in effect until one or more of the following occur:



1\. A real, post-deployment incident demonstrates that this temporal limitation materially prevents accountability from being made explicit.

2\. A regulator formally requests a capability that Rev 7.1 explicitly cannot provide.

3\. The limitation is proven irreducible under all doctrine-preserving approaches.



Absent these conditions, \*\*no new revision is opened\*\*.



---



\## Final Statement



Protocol Qv does not guarantee accountability.



It guarantees that the \*\*presence, absence, or prevention of accountability is explicit, timestamped, and auditable\*\*.



This holding pattern ensures that \*\*deliberate non-action is as reviewable as action itself\*\*.
