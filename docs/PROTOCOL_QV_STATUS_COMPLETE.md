\# Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 — Status: Complete



\## Status Declaration



Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 is \*\*complete\*\*.



It provides a deterministic, post-incident accountability spine that either:

\- produces verifiable accountability artifacts, or

\- explicitly records why accountability could not proceed.



This document is a status declaration, not a roadmap.



---



\## What Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 Does



Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 operates \*\*only after an incident has occurred\*\*.



It:

\- freezes the incident boundary and evidence set,

\- classifies evidence deterministically,

\- records responsibility limits explicitly,

\- accepts or rejects counterfactual claims mechanically,

\- produces a unified incident record,

\- preserves unknowns as first-class artifacts,

\- classifies incident severity from observed outcomes,

\- packages regulator handoff materials,

\- enables cross-incident comparison,

\- provides a public replay path that either reproduces the outcome or emits an explicit refusal.



Each stage produces either:

\- a concrete artifact, or

\- a documented refusal explaining why progression was blocked.



---



\## What Can Be Audited Today



A regulator or third party can independently verify:

\- what evidence was used and when it was frozen,

\- how responsibility was bounded,

\- why certain counterfactuals were rejected,

\- how severity was classified,

\- what information was handed to regulators,

\- whether replay is possible and, if not, the precise reason why.



All outcomes are deterministic and evidence-bound.



---



\## What the Protocol Explicitly Refuses to Do



Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 does \*\*not\*\*:

\- predict future harm,

\- rank individuals,

\- assign intent or blame,

\- optimize decisions,

\- provide design-time governance,

\- proceed when evidence is missing, altered, or blocked,

\- circumvent legal, sovereign, or vendor constraints.



When such conditions occur, the protocol records an explicit refusal.



---



\## Role of Refusal



Refusal is \*\*not failure\*\*.



It is a terminal, auditable outcome that records why accountability could not proceed further.

This prevents silent failure and preserves institutional traceability.



---



\## Scope Boundary



Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 is not:

\- a risk scoring system,

\- a fairness framework,

\- a compliance guarantee,

\- an enforcement mechanism.



It is an accountability documentation system.



---



\## Conditions Under Which Rev 7.2 May Be Opened



Rev 7.2 may be opened \*\*only if\*\* at least one of the following occurs:



\- a real incident cannot be represented without violating Rev 7.1 constraints,

\- a new, previously unclassified accountability failure mode is observed,

\- a GAP is shown to be unenforceable in practice,

\- an existing refusal class proves insufficient to explain a blockage,

\- a regulator formally requests a capability Rev 7.1 explicitly cannot provide.



Absent these conditions, Rev 7.1 remains frozen.



---



\## Final Statement



Protocol Qv Rev 7.1 does not promise accountability.



It guarantees that the \*\*presence, absence, or prevention of accountability is explicit, timestamped, and auditable\*\*.



Nothing more is claimed.
